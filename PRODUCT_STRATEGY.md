# LOVABLE MVP – Product Strategy, Prioritized Feature Set, Problem Framing, KPIs & GTM

*(For Social/Digital Agencies – AI-Native Proposal Intelligence Platform)*

---

# 1. PRODUCT CONTEXT

Social and digital marketing agencies lose massive amounts of non-billable time on:

* Manual client audits (6–12 hours per lead)
* Rewriting proposals from scratch
* Explaining value/ROI to clients
* Creating pricing tiers for different budgets
* Making proposals visually impressive
* Managing follow-ups without insight

The LOVABLE MVP focuses on solving the **highest-friction, highest-frequency** workflows using **AI-native automation**, not by adding enterprise software bloat.

This document outlines:

* Prioritized MVP features (with problem statements)
* Clear value for the user
* KPIs for launch success
* GTM approach to validate the product

---

# 2. PRIORITIZED MVP FEATURES (RICE + PROBLEM-FOCUSED)

Below is a clear MVP feature set with prioritization and problem alignment.

## ✅ **Feature 1: AI Agency Auto-Onboarding** (Highest Priority)

**Problem:** Agencies spend hours building templates, describing services, gathering case studies, creating brand tone.

**Solution:** User enters their website → AI builds:

* Service library
* Tone & brand voice
* Portfolio summaries
* Value propositions
* Default proposal template

**Why High Priority:** Immediate WOW moment. Zero-friction onboarding.

---

## ✅ **Feature 2: AI Client Auto-Audit (Audit-to-Strategy Engine)**

**Problem:** Agencies manually analyze a client's social presence, website, brand, and weaknesses — a massive time sink.

**Solution:** User enters client website → AI generates:

* Client profile
* Pain-point analysis
* Social audit
* Suggested strategy areas
* Opportunity summary

**Why It Matters:** Directly replaces 6–12 hours of non-billable work.

---

## ✅ **Feature 3: Full AI Proposal Generator (Narrative + Scope + Strategy + ROI)**

**Problem:** Writing proposals is repetitive, boring, inconsistent, and time-consuming.

**Solution:** One-click generation:

* Executive summary
* Audit insights
* Strategy deck
* Scope of work
* Deliverables
* Timeline
* ROI/value narrative

**Why It Matters:** Converts manual labor into a 30-second process.

---

## ✅ **Feature 4: Pricing Tier Engine (Retainer + Campaign + Ad Spend)**

**Problem:** Agency pricing is chaotic — mixed retainers, ad spend, packages.

**Solution:** System generates:

* 3 pricing tiers (Good/Better/Best)
* Add-ons
* Market-aligned price recommendations
* Sliders for deliverable quantities

**Why It Matters:** Helps agencies price consistently & close faster.

---

## ✅ **Feature 5: Interactive Web Proposal Page**

**Problem:** PDFs feel outdated and static; agencies need to impress prospects.

**Solution:** A clean, modern proposal page with:

* Pricing toggles
* Add-on selection
* Embedded brand style
* Approval/signing CTA
* Book-a-call button

**Why It Matters:** Higher close rates, better engagement.

---

## ✅ **Feature 6: Basic Engagement Insights + AI Follow-Up Suggestions**

**Problem:** Agencies don't know how prospects interact with proposals.

**Solution:** Track:

* Opens
* Section views
* Time per section
* Most-viewed pricing tier

AI suggests:

* "Prospect viewed pricing tier 2 twice → follow up with budget-flexible message."

**Why It Matters:** Helps convert leads with minimum effort.

---

## ⚪ (Optional MVP+ Feature): Centralized Knowledge Hub

Can store:

* Service descriptions
* Pricing defaults
* Case studies
* Voice & tone preferences

**Problem Solved:** AI output consistency.

---

# 3. OUT OF SCOPE FOR MVP (Intentionally Not Included)

These will slow down validation and are NOT needed now:

* CRM integrations
* Payment processing
* Contract lifecycle management
* Predictive close scoring
* Multi-user roles
* Full analytics dashboards
* Enterprise compliance

These belong in **Post-MVP Roadmap (90–180 days)**.

# 4. USER JOURNEYS (End-to-End Flows)

## Journey A: Create & Send a Proposal

1. User signs up
2. Enters agency website → AI creates profile
3. Click "New Proposal"
4. User enters client website → AI audit
5. Click "Generate Proposal"
6. User tweaks pricing tiers (optional)
7. Publish → Share proposal link
8. Prospect views & interacts
9. User receives engagement insights + suggested follow-up

---

## Journey B: Edit Agency Setup

1. Go to Agency Profile
2. Edit services, tone, pricing presets
3. AI regenerates templates

---

## Journey C: Follow-Up Workflow

1. View proposal analytics
2. Receive AI nudge (e.g., budget concerns detected)
3. Click recommended follow-up message
4. Send email or message externally
